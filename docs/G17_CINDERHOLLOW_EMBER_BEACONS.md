# G17 — Cinderhollow ember beacons

Three small procedural ember beacons now frame Cinderhollow's Ember Gallery, Ore Rim, and Crucible
Chamber approach. They are visual-only landmarks built from existing low-poly instanced geometry
and the existing emissive flame material.

They do not receive taps or block movement, introduce no dynamic lights, assets, rewards, or
server changes, and remain inside the existing mobile rendering budget.

## iPhone acceptance

1. Enter Cinderhollow in portrait orientation and confirm the Ember Gallery beacon is visible from
   the cavern mouth without obscuring the movement area.
2. Travel to the Ore Rim and Crucible Chamber; confirm their beacons make the route readable while
   the ore, combat, and crucible prompts still work normally.
3. Tap around each beacon and confirm only authored interactables receive a route or action.
