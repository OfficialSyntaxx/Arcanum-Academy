# G25 — Ashen Overlook basalt steps

Ashen Overlook's Cliff Path and Ember Vista sit on different terrain heights. The zone now has a
pair of authored **Basalt Steps** waypoints at that transition plus a compact procedural stair.
The route is clearer on a phone while its navigation remains entirely in the existing local,
authoritative zone graph.

## Boundaries

- No rewards, items, gathering, combat, discovery, or diary change.
- No new asset, collision, server runtime, social feature, or hosting requirement.

## iPhone acceptance

1. Enter Ashen Overlook from Cinderhollow in portrait orientation.
2. Follow the path from Cliff Path to Ember Vista.
3. Confirm the basalt stair visibly resolves the terrace edge and the local map lists Basalt Steps.
4. Return to Cinderhollow and confirm the contextual travel panel closes normally.
