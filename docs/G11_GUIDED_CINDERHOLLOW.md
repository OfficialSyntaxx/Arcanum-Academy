# G11 — Guided Cinderhollow expedition

Connect Cinderhollow to the existing journal after **The Foothill Forge**. The Journey tracker guides players to mine 6 Cinder Ore, defeat a Cinderbound Wisp, defeat Cinderheart, and retain its unique Sigil on turn-in.

All objectives remain server-confirmed and solo-obtainable. The phase reuses the journal and Journey tracker, introduces no new runtime assets, and preserves free hosting, Ironman progression, and dormant multiplayer.
