# G51 — Ashen Overlook scenic routes

Ashen Overlook now opens from its Basalt Steps into an Ember Crossroads with three reversible
branches: Ember Vista, Glasswind Shelf, and Watcher's Crown. Every destination remains connected to
the entrance and is validated by the shared navigation graph.

## Boundaries

- This is optional exploration with no reward or progression state.
- No interactables, encounters, gathering nodes, items, XP, or server commands were added.
- The existing reciprocal return portal and Cinderhollow route are unchanged.
- The larger terrace uses the existing terrain representation and asset budget.

## Evidence

- The shared world suite verifies the seven-point route, four-way crossroads, three scenic
  destinations, reciprocal links, bounds, and full spawn connectivity.
