# G30 — Elevation route legend

When a local map contains an authored terrain-height transition, it now displays the compact legend
**“Dashed paths climb or descend.”** The copy is conditional, so flat zones keep their existing
uncluttered map panel.

## iPhone acceptance

1. Open Cinderhollow's local map and confirm the dashed-path legend appears below the map.
2. Open a flat-route area and confirm no empty legend space is shown.
3. Confirm the map remains scrollable and every destination control retains its 48px touch target.
