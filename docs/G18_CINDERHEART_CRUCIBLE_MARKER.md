# G18 — Cinderheart crucible marker

The existing Cinderheart encounter now has a compact procedural stone crucible with an emissive
ember core at its authored location. It gives the Crucible Chamber a clear finale silhouette while
the animated boss model remains the actual combat target.

The marker has no collision, no gameplay state, no new asset, no light, and no effect on target
range, combat outcomes, drops, player power, or rewards.

## iPhone acceptance

1. Reach the Crucible Chamber and confirm the stone-and-ember marker is visible behind/around the
   Cinderheart encounter without hiding the HP or action HUD.
2. Tap Cinderheart and confirm normal pathing, range gating, and combat start.
3. Tap the marker's exposed area and confirm it never creates a false route or action target.
